### Required credit lines

ACS requires specific wording for its copyrighted figures. For `fig-05` and
`fig-06`, the on-page caption should read:

> Reprinted with permission from Song, Shi, Pakhare, Sheldon & Guduru,
> *ACS Appl. Energy Mater.* **8**, 5636 (2025). Copyright 2025 American
> Chemical Society.

Elsevier figures (`fig-07`, `fig-08`) should cite the article in full and link
to the version of record on ScienceDirect. Springer Nature figures (`fig-01`,
`fig-04`) should cite authors, journal, volume, page, year and "Springer
Nature".

The two CC BY 4.0 figures need only attribution and a link to the license.
